What's a fractional FDE //

The missing seat on your deal team.

A forward deployed engineer sits with the team, watches the real work, and builds AI into the systems the business already runs on. The role started at Palantir, and OpenAI, Anthropic and Cursor now run their own FDE teams. Fractional means you get one across your portfolio, without hiring one per company. For your first few deals you don't need a full integration team: a fractional CFO, a deal attorney and a lender cover the paper. Nobody on that list installs the systems.

A new vendor per business
  • Every company gets its own agency, tools and logins
  • Every fix is rediscovered from scratch
  • AI tools get built fast, then fail quietly
  • Nobody measures before or after
  • Know-how leaves with the vendor, and shows up at exit
One fractional FDE
  • One toolkit, built once and deployed on every close
  • Business two starts where business one finished
  • Every tool monitored; a failure alerts a person
  • Baseline first, so every kit has a number
  • Built on your accounts, so a buyer can inherit it
How it works //

Build once. Deploy on every close. Keep it running.

The Bending Spoons idea at your size: buy businesses that share a function (leads to booked jobs, repeat purchases, visits and bookings, client delivery) and one toolkit fits all of them.

  1. AI readiness audit Kickoff · $5,000

    I scan one business's site and stack, check what's being measured, and score every process. You get a ranked plan: what to automate first, what to queue, and what stays by hand. Run it on a company you own, or on a target during diligence.

  2. Baseline

    Before anything is installed, we track the one conversion that matters, so every change has a before and after number.

  3. Deploy the kit

    Tools go onto the systems the team already uses. A person approves anything a customer sees until the tool has earned trust.

  4. Troubleshoot and carry forward

    I fix what breaks, log every correction, and carry the fixes into the next business you buy.

Knowing when //

Nothing jumps straight to AI. Every task earns each step.

Knowing when to automate is the job. And building the tool is only 20% of it. The other 80% is getting your team to trust it, which is the part I focus on.

1 · By hand
A person does it

Move on when it repeats every week and the steps can be written down.

2 · Rules
Plain software

Move on when the fixed steps run clean and what's left needs judgment.

3 · AI + review
AI drafts, a person approves

Move on when it matches a person on real cases with known answers.

4 · AI on its own
Monitored, not supervised

Only when a mistake is cheap and gets caught. Costly calls stay human.

Questions //

Before you book.

What happens after I pay?

You get a confirmation from Stripe, and I follow up by email to schedule the audit and get access to what I need to look at.

Is $5,000 the whole engagement?

The audit is the kickoff and stands on its own: you keep the ranked plan either way. If you want a fractional FDE across your portfolio after that, we scope it from what the audit finds.

What's the difference between the mastermind and the fractional FDE?

In the mastermind I ship one kit a quarter for you, and you learn to run it alongside four other owners. As your fractional FDE, I build, deploy and run the whole toolkit across your portfolio. The audit is the first step for both, and it's credited toward the mastermind.

What is the Acq Scanner?

Your own acquisition scanner, running on the same Jev scoring from the talk. We load your buy box: the functions, size, revenue model and red flags you care about. You scan any target's website, and it scores the fit and ranks what to automate first after close. Every sprint, we tune it from what the last deals taught us.

What if I don't see results?

If your first 90-day sprint doesn't put a measured win on the scoreboard, your next quarter is on me. Every build gets a baseline before it goes in, so the number is agreed up front.

What does exit-ready mean?

Everything is built on your own accounts, documented, and monitored, so a buyer can inherit it without me. You get a one-page summary of the systems, what they do and what they've measured, ready to drop into your CIM.

How do I join the mastermind?

By application. Apply through the Jev intake, then we have a short call to check the fit. There are only five founding seats, so every member gets real time in the room, and applications close once they're filled. If you've already bought the audit, it's credited toward membership.

Can I run it on a business I haven't bought yet?

Yes. Running the audit on a target during diligence shows what pays back first after close, and what could hurt the exit.

Do I need to be technical?

No. My job is to keep the systems simple enough to explain to you, and solid enough to maintain, monitor and fix.

Who owns the tools?

You do. Everything is built on your own accounts, so a future buyer can inherit it cleanly.
