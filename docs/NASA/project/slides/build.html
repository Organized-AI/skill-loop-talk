04 / 10

Every change goes through the same eight steps, and the arcade cabinet acts them out. The coin is the prompt. The coin switch is the approval: nothing runs until the team says yes. The pulse to the board is the agent writing code, and the screen booting up is Vite reloading the page: launch. The most important step is five: the agent asks, the team votes. If it wants to do something we didn’t ask for, we say no and explain why.
