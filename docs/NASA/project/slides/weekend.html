The weekend at ACC Rio Grande. Saturday: doors at 8:30, opening ceremony at 10, hacking starts at 11. There’s a 30-minute warning at 4:30, and at 5 everyone under 18 leaves with their adult and keeps building online from home. Sunday: back at 9 with your yellow card. The two times that matter most are 1:00 PM, when the RiverHacks submission goes in on GitHub, and 11:59 PM, when the NASA global submission closes. The Space Apps page says 3 PM for local submissions, so confirm at kickoff, but plan for 1 PM. Winners are announced around 5 PM.
