Here’s what happens, in less than a second, every time someone presses Launch! Your click goes to the frontend, the page behind door 5173. The frontend sends an order, “get me a fact,” over to the backend behind door 8000. The backend grabs one checked fact from our fact list and sends it back as JSON, which works like a filled-in form with labels. The source travels with the fact, and the page shows both.
