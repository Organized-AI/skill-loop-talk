Here is what happens in under a second when someone presses Launch! The frontend, behind door 5173, sends a request called GET /api/fact. Vite forwards it to the backend behind door 8000. The backend picks one checked fact and sends it back as JSON, a text format people and programs can both read. The source travels with the fact, and the computer’s screen shows the moment it lands on the page.
