— When to automate

Every task earns its way to AI.

STEP 1

A person does it

Next when it repeats every week and the steps are written down.

STEP 2

Simple software does it

Next when the fixed steps run clean and only judgment calls are left.

STEP 3

AI drafts, a person approves

Next when it gets real cases as right as the person does.

STEP 4

AI runs it, watched

Only if a mistake is cheap and gets caught. Costly calls stay with a person.

80/20

Building the tool is 20% of the work. Getting the team to trust it is 80%.

Most people can't flowchart a task they do every week. Start AI read-only. Earn write access.

From Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, on the a16z podcast

— How I use Jev every day

Code reads. Jev judges. A person approves.

CODE DECIDES

The facts

Is every piece connected?

Did a privacy setting change?

Is this still the latest version?

JEV JUDGES

The meaning

Does this change match what was asked?

Does it reach further than it should?

Does the name fit the job?

A PERSON APPROVES

The consequences

Anything a customer sees.

Anything that touches consent or money.

The final yes.

EXAMPLE · YOUR AD TRACKING

Someone edits the purchase tag. Code finds it now fires on 4 pages instead of 1. Jev judges it broader than the request, so it gets sent back. If a privacy setting was loosened, it goes straight to a person, whatever the score.
