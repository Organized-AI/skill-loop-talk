The Jev score

One number. Four dimensions.

score = round(20 × (0.35T + 0.25A + 0.20D + 0.20E))
35%T · Time-to-value
25%A · Automation fit
20%D · Data readiness
20%E · Exit-safety
75+ · INSTALL NOW55–74 · QUEUE NEXTUNDER 55 · PARKExit-safety of 2 or less flags “Rework first.”

Each dimension is scored 1 to 5.

The honest part

The part most demos skip.

Not on Cloudflare yet

The build plan is written and the router is ready to move unchanged into a Worker. The harness itself is not deployed there.

Live scan is owner-only

It uses one person's Context.dev connector. The room needs the Worker scan route with a server-side key.

Tags are not proof

A pixel in the source does not show the event fires. Step zero suggests, and the buyer confirms.

Not yet run in the viewer

Tested with a mock connector and two real scans, not by clicking Scan inside Claude.
