— Evidence, not claims

Said is not shown.

VERIFIED

1.0

A file in your repo does it, and Jev agrees the quote shows it.

SHOWN

0.8

A file does it, and Jev hasn't decided yet.

CLAIMED

0.25

JUDY.md or your Loom says so, or the repo only has a TODO or part of it.

MISSING

0

Nothing in the repo, the form or the video.

Every quote is checked against the file at the audited commit. A quote that isn't there doesn't count, and a note to the auditor hidden in your repo is ignored.

— Where the trial cross-examines the system

Your claims meet your judge's behavior.

CONTRADICTION

You list injection tests. Your judge obeyed 2 of 5 injections.

That practice earns nothing, its pillar stops at Partial, and the team can't be in good standing.

BLIND SPOT

Your judge flipped on 1 of 6 swapped pairs. Nothing in your repo swaps them.

It goes to the top of your next three changes. A high-risk blind spot also blocks good standing.

STATED NUMBERS

You state 99% accuracy. Judy measured 70%.

The test sets differ, so only gaps over 15 points count. A gap that big usually means your own set is too easy.

Examples for illustration, not real teams.
